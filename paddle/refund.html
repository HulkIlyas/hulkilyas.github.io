Refund Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026 | Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Refund Policy outlines the terms and procedures for refund requests regarding purchases and subscriptions made for IDARR — Budget & Expense Manager. We aim to ensure a fair and transparent experience for all customers.

2. Purchases

IDARR offers digital software access and premium feature upgrades ("Pro plans"). Purchases are completed through authorized payment gateways, such as Paddle, or applicable mobile app distribution stores.

3. IDARR Pricing

IDARR provides Pro features under the following pricing structure:

  • Monthly: $2.99 USD / month
  • Yearly: $24.00 USD / year
  • Lifetime: $4.99 USD (one-time payment)

4. Subscription Renewals

Monthly and Yearly plans are auto-renewing subscription services. Subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each billing period unless you cancel prior to the end of the current active cycle.

5. Lifetime Purchase

The Lifetime plan is a single, non-recurring, one-time payment. It does not auto-renew, and no recurring billing charges will follow a Lifetime purchase.

6. Cancellation

You may cancel a recurring subscription at any time to prevent future billing. Cancellation stops future recurring charges, while allowing you to retain access to premium features through the remainder of your active paid billing period.

7. Refund Requests

If you encounter technical issues, service disruptions, or have questions regarding a charge, you can submit a refund inquiry by reaching out to our support team via our official contact page at https://ilyas.help. Please provide your order details or transaction ID to help us assist you promptly.

8. Duplicate or Accidental Charges

In the event of duplicate charges or accidental double purchases, please contact support immediately with your transaction receipts so we can verify the error and process a prompt correction.

9. Payment Provider

Payments and refunds may be processed by Paddle or other authorized payment partners. Consequently, statutory customer rights, transaction terms, currency policies, and refund timelines established by our payment providers apply to your purchase.

10. Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility is evaluated on a case-by-case basis. To maintain fair standards:

  • Refunds are not guaranteed for all purchases and are subject to verification.
  • Refunds may be considered in cases of proven technical incompatibility, duplicate charges, or unauthorized transactions.
  • Purchases made directly through mobile app stores (such as Apple App Store or Google Play) may be subject to the store's respective refund policies and procedures.

11. Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, processing times depend on your payment provider and financial institution. Approved refunds typically take 5 to 10 business days to reflect on your payment statement.

12. Changes to This Refund Policy

We reserve the right to modify or update this Refund Policy at any time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

13. Contact

For refund requests, billing inquiries, or general support, please visit our contact link:

Contact Us — https://ilyas.help
