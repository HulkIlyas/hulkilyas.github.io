Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026 | Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how IDARR — Budget & Expense Manager ("we", "us", or "IDARR") handles information when you use our mobile application and related services. We are committed to safeguarding your personal and financial information.

Our Core Commitment: IDARR does NOT sell users' personal financial information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, and improve our budgeting tools. The types of data processed include information you directly input into the application as well as essential technical data necessary for application operations.

3. Information You Provide

When using IDARR, you voluntarily provide information to facilitate expense management. This includes budget goals, daily balance figures, custom categories, expense notes, and transaction logs.

4. Financial and Transaction Data

IDARR processes financial entries that you voluntarily enter into the app—such as income, expense amounts, budget thresholds, spending categories, and daily ledger entries. This data is processed strictly to generate summaries, budget calculations, and spending insights for your personal view within the app.

5. Account and Authentication Information

If you create an account or sign in to IDARR, we may collect basic account identifiers such as an email address or user ID to authenticate your access, sync data across your devices, and secure your session.

6. Device and Technical Information

To ensure app compatibility and optimal presentation, we may automatically collect standard technical information, such as your mobile device model, operating system version, app version, language preferences, and general system performance data.

7. Analytics and Crash Information

We may process anonymized or aggregated diagnostic data and crash reports to identify software bugs, analyze app performance, and optimize feature usability. These metrics do not reveal your personal identity or financial records.

8. Advertising Information

If free or ad-supported tiers are active within the application, generic advertising parameters or non-sensitive device identifiers may be utilized to deliver non-intrusive advertisements in compliance with standard mobile ad ecosystem guidelines.

9. Payment and Subscription Information

When you purchase a Pro plan (Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime), payment transactions are handled directly by authorized payment service providers (such as Paddle) or mobile app store platforms. IDARR itself does not collect or store full credit card numbers or sensitive payment details on our servers.

10. How We Use Information

We use processed information solely for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Providing and delivering IDARR's budgeting and expense-tracking features.
  • Calculating financial summaries, budget tracking, and daily balance totals.
  • Managing active Pro subscriptions and validating purchases.
  • Diagnosing application crashes and improving overall performance.
  • Responding to customer support requests and inquiries.

11. How We Store Information

Your entered financial information is saved locally on your device and, where account sync features are enabled, stored on secure cloud infrastructure utilizing industry-standard security protocols.

12. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical, administrative, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, disclosure, or alteration. However, no electronic transmission or storage technology can be guaranteed 100% secure.

13. Third-Party Services

IDARR may interface with trusted third-party service infrastructure to support cloud databases, crash diagnostics, analytics, advertising, and subscription billing (such as Paddle). These providers process data on our behalf in compliance with relevant data privacy principles.

14. Data Retention

We retain financial data and account information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the application's functionality. Local data remains on your device until cleared or uninstalled.

15. Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated cloud data at any time. You can submit a data deletion request by visiting our contact page at https://ilyas.help. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes locally stored financial data from your device.

16. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, export, or request deletion of your data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

17. Children's Privacy

IDARR is a general-audience financial tracking application and is not intended for or directed to children under 13 years of age (or the legal age requirement in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

18. International Data Transfers

Information processed by IDARR or our cloud service providers may be hosted and processed in servers located outside your home state or country. We ensure appropriate safeguards are maintained for cross-border data transfers.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

20. Contact

If you have any questions, privacy concerns, or data deletion requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our website:

Contact Us — https://ilyas.help

Financial Disclaimer: Financial information entered into IDARR is used exclusively for personal expense tracking and budgeting features. IDARR does not provide professional financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.
